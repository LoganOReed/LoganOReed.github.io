I am a Ph.D. student in the [Department of Mathematics at Temple University](https://www.math.temple.edu/), advised by [Daniel Szyld](https://faculty.cst.temple.edu/~szyld/). My research is in numerical linear algebra: Krylov subspace methods, matrix and tensor equations, and low-rank tensor computations.

I serve as president of the Temple University SIAM Student Chapter and organize the department's graduate seminar. I also teach; currently I am a teaching assistant for Numerical Analysis.
